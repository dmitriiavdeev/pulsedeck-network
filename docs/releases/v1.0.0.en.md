# PulseDeck 1.0.0

**PulseDeck monitors your servers, websites and network devices.** Get notified when a connection drops or comes back.

**[Download PulseDeck for Windows](https://github.com/dmitriiavdeev/pulsedeck-network/releases/download/v1.0.0/PulseDeck-Setup-1.0.0-x64.exe)**

Windows 10 and 11 · 64-bit · Free

Response charts, event history, Telegram and desktop notifications, and 20 alert sounds with adjustable volume. English and Russian interfaces, light and dark themes. Monitoring continues in the background when you close the window.
